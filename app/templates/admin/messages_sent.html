Turning it on

WhatsApp does not let a website message a number without an account with a provider. Two work here:

  • Fonnte or Wablas — Indonesian, a few tens of thousands of rupiah a month, scan a QR code with the spa's phone and it sends as your normal WhatsApp. Simplest for a spa.
  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API — free to start, but needs business verification, and outside 24 hours of a customer messaging you it only sends pre-approved templates.

Once you have a token, add it to .env on the server and restart:

WA_PROVIDER=fonnte
WA_TOKEN=your-token-here

For Wablas also set WA_API_URL to your Wablas domain. For Meta use WA_PROVIDER=meta with WA_TOKEN and WA_PHONE_ID.
